# FlyCompare monetization — Stage 1 (zero upfront spend)

## Offer
Optional human-assisted personalized travel planning report at an introductory ₹199 per itinerary. Deliver an itinerary outline, user-entered budget estimate, preparation checklist and public flight-search links. No ticketing, visa issuance, live fare guarantee or airline affiliation. Free planner stays free.

## Enquiry process
The public planning-service.html form generates a message entirely in the visitor's browser. The customer copies it to a pre-existing trusted contact channel. No contact destination or payment account is configured; obtain operator consent and verified business contact details before promoting direct orders. Do not collect sensitive identity/payment details in the form.

## Manual order register (keep private, not in public repository)
Order ID | enquiry received | customer contact | requested scope | agreed delivery date | price | written refund terms agreed | payment reference | verified in merchant bank/UPI account | report delivered | follow-up.

## Payment verification
1. Confirm deliverables, timeline, final ₹199 price, accepted payment channel and refund/cancellation terms in writing before asking for payment.
2. Provide only the operator's verified payment details privately; never request a UPI PIN, OTP, card CVV, password, or remote device access.
3. Ask customer for amount and transaction reference/UTR privately. A screenshot is insufficient.
4. Verify actual credit, payee and amount independently in the operator's own bank/UPI merchant transaction history. Mark paid only after confirmation; avoid exposing UTRs publicly.
5. Acknowledge order ID and delivery date, then deliver report through agreed channel. Reconcile records daily.
6. If payment is missing, pending, duplicate or mismatched, do not mark paid; resolve directly with the payment provider and customer.

## Delivery template
Trip summary; dates and traveller count; suggested itinerary; estimated budget (assumptions explicit); flight-search links (customer must verify fares); checklist; exclusions and limitations; date prepared. Avoid promising cheapest fare, confirmed seats, bookings or visas.

## Launch plan
- Days 1–2: Verify link, mobile layout, form validation/copy, offline shell, privacy wording, and operator contact/payment/refund process. Do not announce payment readiness until configured.
- Days 3–7: Share a sample anonymized planning report and link organically on existing channels. Ask for voluntary feedback; no spam or fabricated testimonials.
- Week 2: Handle first 5 qualified enquiries manually; track enquiry-to-paid conversion and time spent per report.
- Weeks 3–4: Iterate offer and delivery template from feedback. Target 5 paid reports × ₹199 = ₹995 gross before costs; not a guarantee.
- Later: Apply to legitimate travel affiliate programmes, disclose tracked links and use them only after approval. Do not misrepresent fare availability.
