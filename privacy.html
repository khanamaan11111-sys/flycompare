Privacy & data

FlyCompare is an independent planning tool. It does not sell tickets, provide live fares, or confirm reservations.

Data saved in your browser

FlyCompare uses browser local storage for recent trip searches, checklist progress and saved budget drafts. These values stay in that browser under normal operation. You can clear saved travel data using the control in the main app, or through your browser settings. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove saved drafts.

What you enter

Routes, dates, passenger counts, quotations and budget figures are entered by you. The planning calculations run in your browser; FlyCompare does not require an account or a server-side user profile.

External services

Opening a flight-search provider leaves FlyCompare. That provider has its own privacy practices. The website also requests Google Fonts; your browser may contact Google to load them. Hosting and delivery through GitHub Pages may involve infrastructure-level request logs governed by GitHub policies.

Exports and sharing

Downloading, printing, copying or sharing a plan creates content on your device or sends it through the service you choose. Review personal travel details before sharing. This page describes the application as implemented; it is not a promise about third-party services.
